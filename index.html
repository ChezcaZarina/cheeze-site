Quick answers

Why did you make this website?

I wanted to create something of my own while learning along the way. Basically, a little personal project where I could experiment, try things out, and see what happens. And well... here we are!

Do you know how to code?

Enough to understand the stuff I build. But if you ask me to present my code and explain every little detail, you’ve officially lost me. I know what I did... mostly.

Is this website finished?

Depends on what my mind decides at night. One day it’s finished, the next day I’m suddenly changing everything. So, let’s just say it’s a work in progress.

Can I click everything?

That’s literally why they’re there! Go ahead, click around and see what happens. Just don’t expect every single thing to work perfectly. I’m still learning, okay?

Will there be more updates?

If my motivation cooperates, absolutely. New ideas are welcome. Actually finishing them is a different story.

What’s the point of this website?

Good question... even I had to think about that. HAHAHA. It’s a little space where I can put the things I make, try random ideas, and have fun learning something new. No big mission or anything. I just wanted to make something that feels like mine.
